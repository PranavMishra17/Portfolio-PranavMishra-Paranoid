Now — April 2026 to present

What I'm doing at Alfred_

Alfred_ is a consumer AI executive assistant — a multi-agent system that runs people's email, calendar, and daily obligations over text, chat, and voice. We have more than five thousand active subscribers.

I own the reliability layer: risk-scored gating, an eval harness I built from scratch, a scanner that reads live conversations for production failures, and a rebuilt working memory that makes the assistant's fabrication structurally impossible — enforced by tests, not by hoping the model behaves.

I also built the email-rules engine, the product's stickiest feature. I replaced per-message model calls with a deterministic three-stage matcher: about 30% cheaper per user, and nearly 98% of rules now get created just by talking to it.

Notification latency went from about 90 seconds to about 3. The security and one-time-password path went from 189 seconds at the 90th percentile to instant.

Every action the agent wants to take passes through a five-verdict router I designed — silent, notify, confirm, clarify, refuse — backed by deterministic risk scoring, a queue of pending obligations, and a bounded window to undo.

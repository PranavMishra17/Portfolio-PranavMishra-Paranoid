Now

Founding LLM Engineer at Alfred_, in New York. Alfred_ manages email, calendars, and the small daily obligations for more than five thousand people, over text, chat, and voice.

I built the layer that keeps it honest — risk scoring, an eval harness from scratch, a scanner that reads live conversations for failures, and a working memory that makes the assistant structurally unable to invent facts.

I replaced per-message model calls in the rules engine with a deterministic three-stage match. It costs about thirty percent less, and nearly all new rules now get created just by talking to it.

An email used to take ninety seconds to reach a phone. Now it takes three. A security code used to take three minutes. Now it's instant.

Every action the agent wants to take passes through one of five verdicts before it happens — stay silent, notify, ask first, clarify, or refuse.

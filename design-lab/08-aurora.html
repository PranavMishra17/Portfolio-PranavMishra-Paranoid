Now

New York City. April 2026 to present.

Alfred_ is a multi-agent assistant that manages people's inbox and calendar over text, chat and voice. I own the parts that keep it honest: risk-scored gating before it acts, an eval harness I built from nothing, a scanner that reads live conversations for failures, and a working memory that makes fabrication structurally impossible, not just unlikely.

I also rebuilt how it learns rules. It used to call a model on every message. Now a deterministic three-stage matcher does it, about thirty percent cheaper per user, and it gets nearly all of its new rules just from being talked to.

Every action the agent wants to take passes through a router with five outcomes, silent, notify, confirm, clarify, refuse, scored deterministically rather than judged by the model itself. Notification latency dropped from ninety seconds to about three. The security path went from over three minutes to instant.

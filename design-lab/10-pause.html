Founding LLM Engineer

Alfred_, New York

Alfred_ is a consumer AI executive assistant. It manages email, calendars, and daily obligations for more than five thousand active subscribers, over text, chat, and voice.

I own the reliability layer: risk-scored gating, an eval harness built from scratch, a production scanner that reads live conversations for failures, and a working memory that makes fabrication structurally impossible — enforced by tests, not by hoping the model behaves.

I rebuilt the email-rules engine, the product's stickiest feature, replacing per-message model calls with a deterministic three-stage matcher. About thirty percent cheaper per user, and nearly all new rules now get created just by talking to it.

Notification latency went from about ninety seconds to three. The security-code path went from three minutes at the ninetieth percentile to instant.

Every action the agent wants to take passes through a five-verdict router first — stay silent, notify, ask, clarify, or refuse — decided by deterministic risk scoring, not the model judging itself.

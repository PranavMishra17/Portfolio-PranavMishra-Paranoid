Founding LLM Engineer
Alfred_, New York City

Alfred_ is a consumer AI executive assistant — a multi-agent system that manages people's email, calendar, and daily obligations over SMS, web chat, and voice. It has more than 5,000 active subscribers right now.

  • The reliability layer. Risk-scored gating, an eval harness built from scratch, a scanner that reads live conversations for production failures, and a rebuilt working memory that makes the model fabricating things structurally impossible — enforced by tests, not by hoping it behaves.
  • The email-rules engine, the product's stickiest feature. I replaced per-message model calls with a deterministic three-stage matcher: about 30% cheaper per user, and roughly 98% of rules now get created just by talking to it.
  • Latency. Email-to-SMS notification went from about 90 seconds to about 3. The security and one-time-code path went from 189 seconds at the 90th percentile to instant.
  • The Execution Decision Layer. Every action the agent wants to take passes through a router with five verdicts — silent, notify, confirm, clarify, refuse — decided by a deterministic risk score, not the model judging itself, with a pending-obligations queue and a bounded window to undo.

2023

Bengaluru

The sky sat low and warm most days, the kind of humidity that doesn't move. I was a software developer intern at Bipolar Factory, learning what a system does once it leaves a laptop screen and meets real conditions.

Chicago

Lake-effect grey for most of the year. At UIC's V-ARE Labs I started building virtual patient systems in Unreal Engine 5, and an audio pipeline that, on its clearest readings, classifies correctly 98.52 percent of the time. Both are still running.

Readings taken along the way

July 2025 – March 2026

Charlotte, remote

A quieter stretch. AI Engineer at WheelPrice, working from home most days, the kind of weather that doesn't make the log interesting but keeps everything else running.

April 2026, present

New York City

Clear and holding. Founding LLM Engineer at Alfred_, a multi-agent assistant that manages people's email and calendar over text, chat and voice, now with five thousand active subscribers. I own the reliability layer: risk-scored gating before it acts, an eval harness built from nothing, a scanner that reads live conversations for failures, and a working memory that makes fabrication structurally impossible.

I replaced per-message model calls in the rules engine with a deterministic three-stage matcher, about thirty percent cheaper per user, and it now learns almost all of its rules just from being talked to. Every action the agent wants to take passes through a router with five outcomes, silent, notify, confirm, clarify, refuse, scored deterministically rather than judged by the model itself. Notification latency dropped from ninety seconds to about three. The security path went from over three minutes to instant.

Pranav Pushkar Mishra

Founding LLM Engineer at Alfred_, in New York City. Computer science, University of Illinois Chicago. From Metuchen, New Jersey.

He builds the parts of AI systems that have to be right — the unglamorous parts: what a system is allowed to do, how fast it responds, and whether it is telling the truth.

He is now the founding LLM engineer at Alfred_, a consumer assistant that manages email, calendars and daily obligations for more than five thousand people over text message, chat and voice. Before that: computer vision at WheelPrice, virtual patients in Unreal Engine at a university research lab, and a run of games and virtual worlds that taught him more about performance than any course did.

He once built a museum you could walk through, its paintings hung on a chain. This page borrows the format, not the metaphor: a wall panel at the door, then the work.

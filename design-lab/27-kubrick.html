Part one

The room he is in now

Alfred_ runs five thousand people’s email, calendar and daily obligations over text message, chat and voice — a consumer assistant that answers in the place you already are. April 2026 to now.

A model call sat on every single message. It was replaced with a deterministic matcher, and 98% of the rules that drive it are now made by chatting. Working memory was rebuilt so that it cannot fabricate a fact about your inbox — a property enforced by tests, not by hope.
